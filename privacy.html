Privacy Policy

How VaishnaviStays handles enquiry details shared through this website.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry form or contact us via phone or WhatsApp, we collect only the details you voluntarily provide—such as your name, mobile number, optional email address, preferred villa, stay dates, guest count, and any stay notes.

How We Use Your Details

  • To check villa availability and share tariff quotes for your requested dates.
  • To coordinate booking confirmations and arrival instructions.
  • To answer questions about villa amenities and stay suitability.

We do not sell, rent, or distribute guest contact information to third-party advertisers.

Forms & Third-Party Services

Enquiries submitted on our deployed site are processed via Netlify Forms or transmitted directly via your chosen WhatsApp or telephone application. Embedded regional maps are served by Google Maps and subject to Google's privacy practices.

Questions or Removal Requests

To update or request deletion of enquiry details you previously shared, contact VaishnaviStays directly at +91 9767674307.
