How do I book a villa at VaishnaviStays?

Booking is simple and direct! Message us on WhatsApp at +91 9767674307 or call +91 9767674307. Share your preferred dates, villa choice, and number of guests — we will confirm availability and share the direct booking quote right away.

Which villas are included in the VaishnaviStays collection?

Our collection features 7 villas across Lonavala & Khandala: Lonavala Villa (4BHK with private pool & rain dance), Nayak Villa (modern interiors), Muscut House (terrace view), Khandala Villa (5-star villa with large pool), Captain Villa 7 (spacious garden), Captain Villa 8 (bonfire setup), and Captain Villa 10 (nature retreat).

Are the swimming pools private?

Yes! Unlike shared hotel pools, our pool villas offer private pool access reserved exclusively for your booked stay. Lonavala Villa also features a rain dance area, and Captain Villa 8 includes a waterfall-feature pool.

Can we host birthday parties, reunions, or celebrations?

Yes — our villas are family and party friendly, making them great for birthdays, reunions, anniversaries, and weekend getaways. Please mention your celebration details when enquiring so we can recommend the best property (such as Lonavala Villa, Khandala Villa, or Captain Villa 8 with bonfire setup).

What amenities do the villas offer?

Depending on the villa selected, amenities include private swimming pools, air-conditioned rooms, spacious living halls, dining areas and kitchens, gardens/lawns, terraces, rain dance (Lonavala Villa), bonfire setups (Captain Villa 8), and scenic mountain view settings. View each villa's details on our Our Villas page.

How are check-in/check-out timings and group capacities handled?

Exact check-in and check-out timings as well as comfortable guest capacities vary depending on the villa and your booking dates. Please check with our booking desk at +91 9767674307 before confirming so everything aligns with your schedule and group size.

What about food, kitchen access, and meals during the stay?

Dining arrangements and kitchen access depend on the specific property selected. When you message us on WhatsApp at +91 9767674307, let us know your meal preferences so we can advise on kitchen access or local meal options for your villa.

How far are Lonavala and Khandala from Mumbai and Pune?

Lonavala and Khandala sit right along the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, making them convenient weekend escapes from both Mumbai and Pune — typically around 1.5 to 2.5 hours by road depending on your departure point and traffic.

What are the cancellation, refund, and security deposit policies?

Booking, cancellation, rescheduling, and any refundable security deposit terms vary by villa and dates (especially peak weekends and holidays). All terms are shared transparently prior to confirmation when you enquire via +91 9767674307.

Are parking or pet-friendly stays available?

Parking capacity and pet policies differ from property to property. Please inform us of your vehicle count or if you plan to travel with pets while enquiring so we can match you with the right villa.

When is the best season to visit Lonavala & Khandala?

Lonavala and Khandala are enjoyable year-round: the monsoon months bring lush greenery, mist, and waterfalls; winter brings crisp, pleasant evenings great for bonfires; and summer is refreshing for private pool getaways.
