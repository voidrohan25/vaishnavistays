Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on direct villa bookings, amenities, celebrations, and weekend stays in Lonavala & Khandala.

How do I book a villa at VaishnaviStays?

Call or message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 9767674307 with your preferred villa, dates, and group size. We confirm live availability and direct tariff rates with no middleman fees.

Which villas are in the VaishnaviStays collection?

We offer 7 standalone villas across Lonavala & Khandala: four 4 BHK villas (Lonavala Villa, Nayak Villa, Muscut House, and Khandala Villa) and three 3 BHK villas (Captain Villa 7, Captain Villa 8, and Captain Villa 10).

Are the swimming pools private?

Yes. Unlike shared hotel pools, our pool villas offer dedicated pool access for your booked stay. Lonavala Villa also includes a rain dance setup and Captain Villa 8 features a waterfall pool.

Can we host family gatherings, birthdays, or celebrations?

Yes, our villas welcome families, friends, and celebratory stays. Please mention your occasion while enquiring so we can suggest the most suitable property (such as Lonavala Villa, Khandala Villa, or Captain Villa 8 with bonfire setup).

What are the check-in, check-out, and guest capacity rules?

Exact check-in/check-out timings and maximum guest capacity vary by property and booking dates. Please verify the exact schedule for your chosen villa at +91 9767674307 prior to confirmation.

How are meals and kitchen access handled?

Kitchen access and local meal arrangements depend on the specific property you select. Share your food preferences when enquiring so we can confirm options upfront.

How far are Lonavala and Khandala from Mumbai and Pune?

Both destinations sit along the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, making them accessible weekend getaways typically around 1.5 to 2.5 hours by road depending on your starting neighborhood and traffic.

What about cancellation, rescheduling, and security deposits?

Cancellation terms, rescheduling flexibility, and any refundable security deposit depend on the individual property and stay dates. All terms are stated transparently before you confirm.

Are parking and pets accommodated?

Parking spaces and pet friendliness vary across properties. Please share your vehicle count or pet travel plans when reaching out at +91 9767674307.
